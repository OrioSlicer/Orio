# Roadmap

What is coming to Orio. Priorities move with your feedback: upvote the ideas you want in [Discussions > Ideas](https://github.com/OrioSlicer/Orio/discussions/categories/ideas).

## Next

- **Settings as illustrated cards.** Every group of settings in a small illustrated window, like the scarf seam and wave overhang windows today: see what a value does before you slice.
- **More PrusaSlicer printers and filaments.** The models of PrusaSlicer's catalog that OrcaSlicer does not have yet.
- **Layer fusion view, part 3.** Figures in columns, with a short explanation of each value.
- **Line-type cards, parts 2 and 3.** More settings and drawings in the "i" cards of the preview legend.
- **Translations of this page.** The README and guides in every language Orio speaks.

## Later

- **macOS version.**
- **Tilted layers and G-code Drawing out of beta.**
- **Mesh to STEP out of beta** in the Converter tab.
