# Credits

Orio exists thanks to the work of many people, released as open source.

| Project | Authors | What Orio owes it |
|---|---|---|
| [Bambu Studio](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio) | Bambu Lab and contributors | The base of Orio: interface, slicing engine, Bambu Lab printer support, profiles (AGPL-3.0) |
| [PrusaSlicer](https://github.com/prusa3d/PrusaSlicer) | Prusa Research and contributors | Bambu Studio's foundation; the arrange engine (arr2), 3D honeycomb infill, duplicate / instance (AGPL-3.0) |
| [Slic3r](https://github.com/slic3r/Slic3r) | Alessandro Ranellucci and the RepRap community | Where it all started (AGPL-3.0) |
| [OrcaSlicer](https://github.com/SoftFever/OrcaSlicer) | SoftFever and contributors | Multi-material tab, flow ratios by line type, alternate extra wall, make overhangs printable, infill patterns (quarter cubic, lateral honeycomb, TPMS-D, TPMS-FK), Emboss text tool, printer and filament catalog (AGPL-3.0) |
| [Unlayered Slicer](https://github.com/Unlayered3D/Unlayered-Slicer) | Unlayered3D and contributors | Wave overhangs (AGPL-3.0) |

Orio also relies on the libraries these projects use, among others wxWidgets, Boost, CGAL, OpenCASCADE, OpenVDB, TBB, Clipper, Eigen, libigl, Dear ImGui, nlohmann/json, cURL, OpenSSL, FFmpeg components. Each one is under its own license, listed in the source tree.

Printer and filament profiles come from Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer and their contributors, including the manufacturers who maintain them.
